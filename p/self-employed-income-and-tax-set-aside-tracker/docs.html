Documentation

Everything Self-Employed Income and Tax Set-Aside Tracker does, and what it deliberately does not do.

Getting started

Open the tool, fill in the fields, and use the result. There is no account, no install and no export step to learn.

How the set-aside is worked out

Money in, minus what you spent on the business, gives your profit. Take off the tax-free amount, then apply your tax rate and any other contributions. What is left over is yours.

Change the numbers to match where you live

The tax-free amount and the rates are just fields. Set them to your own country's numbers once and the tool follows them. The defaults are a common UK setup, not a rule.

Why the percentage matters more than the total

The habit that works is moving a fixed share of every payment the day it lands. The tool tells you what that share is, so you never have to think about it again.

This is not your tax return

It is a working estimate to keep you covered. It does not know about other income, allowances you may be due, or anything unusual in your situation.
