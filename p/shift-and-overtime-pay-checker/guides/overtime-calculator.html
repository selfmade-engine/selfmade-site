The short answer

Payslips are short by half an hour here and a break there, and people have no way to show it. Shift and Overtime Pay Checker works it out for you, free, in your browser — nothing to install and nothing uploaded.

How the hours are worked out

Finish time minus start time, minus any unpaid break. If you finished after midnight it still counts properly - a shift from 22:00 to 06:00 is eight hours, not minus sixteen.

Why it is worth doing at all

People paid by the hour - hospitality, care, retail, warehouse, agency work. put this off because it feels like admin rather than work. It is not: it is the difference between knowing where you stand and guessing at it, and the guess is always more flattering than the truth.

Doing it without a tool

You can do all of this on paper or in a spreadsheet, and plenty of people do. The cost is not the arithmetic, it is remembering to do it and keeping the same method every time. A month you forget is a month missing from the total, and you find out about it at the worst possible moment.
