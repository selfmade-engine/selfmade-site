Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

The short version

The tools run in your browser. What you type into them stays on your own device and is never sent to us. We hold an email address only if you make an account, and payment details never touch our systems at all.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter into a calculator or tracker — figures, dates, names, notes — is held in your own browser's local storage. It is not uploaded, not backed up by us, and not readable by us. Clearing your browser data deletes it permanently, and we cannot recover it for you.

What we hold

  • If you make an account: your email address, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password. We cannot read your password.
  • If you subscribe: the fact that you have an active subscription, its start and renewal date, and the email address it belongs to.
  • Anonymous page counts: how many times a page was opened and which site linked to it. No names, no accounts, no profile, no tracking between sites.

Payment details

Card numbers are entered on a page served by Stripe and go directly to them. They never pass through our pages or our servers, and we never see or store them. Stripe tells us only that a payment succeeded, for which product, and the email address used.

Email

We send email for one reason: things you asked for. A confirmation code when you sign up, a receipt when you are charged, and a link if you ask to reset your password. We do not send marketing email and we do not sell, rent or share your address.

Who else is involved

  • Stripe — takes payments and stores card details.
  • Cloudflare — runs the service that holds accounts and subscriptions.
  • GitHub Pages — serves these pages.
  • Google (Gmail) — delivers the email we send you.

No one else receives your data, and it is never sold.

Cookies

No advertising or tracking cookies are used. Your browser's local storage holds your sign-in so you are not asked for a password on every visit, and remembers your own working data.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it entirely. Email selfmade.engine@gmail.com and it will be done. Deleting your account removes your address, your password hash and your subscription record; we keep the minimum transaction record that tax law requires us to keep.

Children

These tools are meant for adults running their own work or finances, and are not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about any of this: selfmade.engine@gmail.com.
